Privacy Policy

Cassatie of Cel · Cassation or Cell

Effective date: 13 July 2026

Short version: Cassatie of Cel collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it sends nothing over the internet. Everything you do stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Cassatie of Cel (also published as “Cassation or Cell”, “Cassation ou Cachot”, and “Kassation oder Knast”) is a game developed and published by Verdict Studios. You can reach us at info@verdictstudios.be.

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, request, or transmit any personal data. There is no sign-up, no login, no account, and no user profile. We never ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any identifier.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you play, the app saves a small amount of information locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage:

  • your game progress and saved run (so you can resume),
  • your local high-score table,
  • your settings (chosen language, sound on/off).

This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. No network, no third parties

The app works fully offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics or measurement SDKs, and no third-party services that could collect data. It does not use cookies and makes no network requests of its own. (Like most apps built on a standard app framework, the Android version declares the technical internet permission; the app never uses it to send or fetch data.) The app may use your device’s vibration (haptics) for feedback; this happens entirely on the device and involves no data.

One exception, and only if you choose it: case outcomes cite the Belgian statute involved, and tapping such a citation opens the law text in your device’s browser, outside the app (on wetwijzer.be, lisloi.be, gesetzguide.be or lexlibera.be). Nothing is opened or transmitted unless you tap the link, and those websites are separate from the app and have their own privacy notices.

5. Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages with respect to privacy. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. You remain in full control of the on-device data described in section 3 and can erase it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Effective date” above. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s store listing.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@verdictstudios.be.
